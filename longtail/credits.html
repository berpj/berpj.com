Longtail After Dark

A night on Bangkok’s Chao Phraya, from Wat Arun to Taksin Bridge beside The River condominium. Made for quick fares, close calls and big wakes.

The river

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under ODbL 1.0. The bundled river centreline, shoreline, measured map widths and landmark footprints were retrieved from OpenStreetMap on 10 October 2026.

The course follows real geography. The shore is smoothed for driving, boarding berths are positioned for play, and all buildings and boats are authored game approximations. This is an arcade game, not a navigation chart.

Music

“Exit the Premises” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com). Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Original recording duration 3:30, ISRC USUAN1500029. Compressed to 128 kbps MP3 for this game; the composition and arrangement are unchanged.

Music begins only after you press Make Waves. It can be switched off separately in the pause menu. Master mute silences music, engine, horn and effects. Boat engine, water, horns and game effects are original procedural audio.

Buildings & boats

Original procedural models: Wat Arun, Wat Pho, Wat Kalayanamit, Santa Cruz, Holy Rosary, River City, ICONSIAM, The River, Mandarin Oriental, State Tower, Memorial Bridge, Chao Phraya Sky Park and Taksin Bridge. Boat models include wooden long-tails, dinner cruises, orange-flag ferries, electric river taxis and one-person fishing boats.

Riverside shophouse texture reused from the Bangkok Tuk Tuk project’s generated artwork. Asset provenance. No Street View, map tiles or third-party building photographs are bundled. Landmark and place names identify locations; the game is independent and unaffiliated with their operators.
