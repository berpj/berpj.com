Real streets.
Original arcade spirit.

A local browser game built with Three.js, Rapier, TypeScript and original procedural art. Bangkok's street layout, building footprints and elevated railway alignment come from OpenStreetMap.

Open map data

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Map data is available under the Open Database License. The game’s road widths, building heights and architectural detailing include authored estimates. Gameplay anchors are fictional.

Download the game geography database · Source and provenance · Data quality report

Materials and artwork

Surface textures from Poly Haven, licensed CC0. Asset credits, source links and hashes · Additional surface maps. Tuk-tuk, traffic, characters, shop signs and street furniture are original code-generated meshes. The shophouse facade texture is original AI-generated artwork; its generation prompt and provenance are included. Six additional shop textures (massage, barber, tailor, pizza, laundry and vacant shutters) were made with built-in image generation; their complete prompts and provenance are included. Business names and placements are fictional. Environmental art is a stylized interpretation of Bangkok, rather than a photographically exact reconstruction.

Open-source libraries

Three.js, MIT · Rapier, Apache 2.0 · Vite, MIT · TypeScript, Apache 2.0

polygon-clipping, MIT, used to keep building footprints clear of drivable roads. Clipper, Boost Software License, is used offline to bake non-overlapping road surfaces.

Sound and typography

Original synthesized engine, horn, effects and music using Web Audio. Barlow typeface by Jeremy Tribby, licensed under the SIL Open Font License. The game is inspired by arcade taxi-driving mechanics and uses original branding and assets.

Local records stay in this browser. No account, live map service or paid API is required.
